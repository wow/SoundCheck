# SoundCheck — product definition

Status: v0.1 scope fixed 2026-09-24. Open source. Source of truth for scope and UX.

## One sentence
SoundCheck levels a DJ library to one loudness and makes new tracks start exactly on the grid, so rekordbox, Serato and Traktor agree with it, without touching the sound or losing a single tag.

## Who it is for
1. **DJs** preparing new tracks and USB exports (rekordbox/CDJ, Serato, Traktor, Engine DJ) and levelling libraries they already cue-pointed.
2. **Producers and edit makers** who bounce at inconsistent levels and want a track that drops into rekordbox or Ableton at bar 1 with an exact BPM.
3. Streaming/podcast users (secondary): integrated-loudness presets.

## The three promises (and their tests)
1. **Same loudness** — gain only; when the ceiling would be hit the row says "Short by X LU". Test: EBU Tech 3341/3342 conformance; within 0.1 LU of ffmpeg `ebur128`.
2. **Fits the grid in rekordbox** — after export, rekordbox 7's own fresh analysis puts beat 1 at 0.000 s (+/-2 ms) with the same 2-decimal BPM on >= 18/20 tracks of the owner's labelled set; wrong tracks are fixed in the app in < 30 s.
3. **Nothing lost** — every tag block and the cover art carried byte-for-byte (proven by per-block SHA-256 fixtures and shown on screen), originals backed up, Revert available, filenames unchanged.

## What v0.1 does
1. **Analyse** WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3 (M4A/AAC/ALAC, Ogg/Opus analyse-only): loudness (I, max-M, max-S, **S-P95**, S-top30, LRA, true peak, PLR, timeline), beats/downbeats/BPM (0.01) with a three-state confidence and reason chips, **meter with grouping** (4/4, 3/4, 6/8, 9/8 aksak 2+2+2+3, 5/8, 7/8, 10/8), static-vs-drifts verdict with residual statistics, spec (rate/depth/channels/codec, DJ-safe flag), tags and cover art (read).
2. **Loudness fix** behind a top-level **DJ | Streaming** switch. DJ: one fixed, persisted **DJ target** on S-P95 (default -11 until measured; "Calibrate from my library" sets it once) and **Club hot** -8. Streaming: Spotify -14, Apple -16 on Integrated. Targets can be set from -30 to -4 LUFS and ceilings from -6 to 0 dBTP. Gain only, ceiling-capped (-0.5 dBTP DJ, -1.0 streaming), "Short by X LU" when headroom runs out; MP3 via lossless `global_gain` in 1.5 dB steps with the residual shown. Per-track override.
3. **Grid inspect and fix** per track (`Enter` or a double-click on an analysed row opens it, `Esc` returns with the row selected): waveform at beat zoom auto-located to bar 1 (the original in grey, the planned gain lighter inside it), bar ruler numbering every beat or pulse when zoomed in (9/8 2+2+2+3 reads `1 2 3 2 5 2 7 2 3`), grid overlay, residual lane (per bar / per beat), an overview strip of the whole track that moves the view, a cursor readout (time, bar and beat or pulse and group, residual, short-term loudness), the fit with its numbers coloured against the verdict limits, drift card with numbers; while playing, the waveform scrolls under the playhead once it reaches the middle of the view (with macOS Reduce motion it pages instead); in a window narrower than 1000 px the rail folds into a one-line fit strip above the waveform (confidence, BPM, meter, P95 and max, verdict) with the rest behind Details (`I`), and the toolbars keep their icons (Before / after, Beat 1 here) and the time signature; keys: `Space` play (from one beat before bar 1 the first time), `Home` back to bar 1, `C` click, `A` the analysed grid dashed under the edited one, `D` bar 1 at the pointer, `,` / `.` nudge, `1`-`9`, `T` tap, `M` meter, `R` reset, `Cmd+Z` / `Cmd+Shift+Z`, arrows pan, `+` / `-` zoom, `Enter` confirm and open the next Needs-review track, `N` next without confirming, `B` original / processed, `Shift+B` match level, `Shift+Up` / `Shift+Down` volume +/-1 dB; fixes: drag anchor (kick-snapped), nudge +/-1 ms (10 ms, 1 beat), type/tap BPM, x2 / /2, `1`-`n` set which beat is beat 1, **fit to the whole track or its start** (offered when the tempo changes within the track: Start fits the grid to the opening from the first kick, up to 128 beats and fewer when the tempo already changes within them, where a DJ mixes in, each option showing the share of kicks it holds there; the rest still reads Drifts), meter picker (runner-up first), re-fit from here, reset, undo; ruler and click accents follow the grouping; **click audition** (accented downbeat) on a minimal in-app player, which plays the track at its planned gain (a settings change applies to the open track at once; the click follows that level and stays 6 dB over the music). **Live meters** either side of the waveform, its full height: IN (the original) on the left, OUT (at the planned gain, what an export holds) on the right, 40 px wide (16 px in a window narrower than 1000 px, the numbers then in a tooltip), one scale from -36 to +3 dB with ticks every 6 dB; the bar is the true peak (dBTP) with an instant attack, coloured in fixed zones (green below -12 dB, yellow from -12 to -3 dB, red above -3 dB), a peak-hold tick that holds 1.5 s then falls 20 dB per 1.7 s, and the momentary loudness (400 ms, LUFS) as a tick with a small arrow; the held peak and the momentary loudness are written above each bar in 12 px numbers, their units in the tooltip; OUT also draws the loudness target (dashed: the S-P95 target in DJ mode, the integrated target in Streaming mode) and the true-peak ceiling; a peak over the ceiling (OUT) or over 0 dBTP (IN) turns the peak readout and tick red until clicked or another track opens; stopped, the bars are empty and the strips show the track's numbers instead (IN `S-P95 -9.2 · TP -0.3`, OUT the planned values, Integrated in Streaming mode); OUT always reads IN plus the planned gain, whatever is heard and whatever the volume. **A/B audition** in the transport: `Original | Processed -2.3 dB` switches what is heard at the same position without a gap (a 10 ms ramp; every opened track starts on Processed), the meter of the version heard at full brightness and the other dimmed; **Match level** plays both at the same loudness (with gain only the two then sound the same; it matters once the limiter changes the sound); the click follows the switch. **Volume** right of play/pause: a 96 px slider (an icon with a popover in a window narrower than 1000 px, or when the transport row is too narrow for it), 0 dB at the right end, -60 dB then off at the left, the level shown on hover and while dragging, `Shift`+drag for fine steps, the speaker icon mutes; it only sets what is heard (not the meters, the planned gain or any file) and is remembered across tracks and restarts. A stereo file longer than 20 minutes plays as mono (left and right averaged), and its meter readings are marked as such. Edits are saved (under `~/Library/Application Support/app.soundcheck.desktop/grid-edits`, so they survive restarts and a cleared cache) and apply while the audio is unchanged (a DJ app writing tags keeps them; another master of the track drops them). A grid the user confirms by ear leaves the Needs-review queue; its drift, residual and BPM-range marks stay on the row, and should a new analysis give a different grid for the same edit, the track returns to the queue.
4. **Two modes**: **Prepare** (new tracks; lossless files cut at the head so the first bar line lands a lead after the start (5 ms until calibrated against rekordbox; the bar line is bar 1 or one extrapolated from it by whole bars at the written two-decimal BPM), removing at most the bar's last beat ("Cut 0.21 s"); the cut moves back up to 1 ms to the quietest moment, so the bar line lands up to 1 ms past the lead, and the first 2 ms fade in from silence, so the cut never clicks; a file whose first bar line already lies before the lead is not cut, since that would mean adding silence ("Starts on bar 1" when that line is bar 1 itself, else "Starts on a bar line (bar 1 at 8.00 s)"); when the first bar line lies more than a beat after the lead, nothing is cut ("Not cut: bar 1 1.00 s in"; "Not cut: first bar line 1.00 s in (bar 1 at 9.00 s)") and the grid travels in tags and the XML, because cutting more would remove music; a track whose grid needs review is not cut until the user confirms its grid by ear ("Not cut: grid needs review"), because a cut at an untrusted bar 1 would start the file in the wrong place; its gain and loudness tags are still written, but not its tempo or grid (no BPM tag, and the `SOUNDCHECK` record says `bpm=none;bar1=none`), so a BPM tag that disagreed with the grid stays and the track still asks for review after the export; Library mode does not write such a grid either, and the row says the BPM and bar 1 were withheld; "grid only" skips such a track ("its grid needs review"), because it would write nothing but an empty record; the cut shown, the frame count and the bar 1 recorded in the file are planned from where the cut actually falls after the 1 ms move; in place allowed; Serato data is detected when a file is added (ID3 `GEOB` objects described `Serato ...` in WAV, AIFF and MP3, `SERATO_*` Vorbis fields in FLAC; M4A is not read): an in-place cut of such a file is refused, because its Serato cue points are positions in the audio and would move (also when the file's tags cannot be read to rule Serato data out), while a cut copy in another folder keeps its Serato cue points and beat grid byte for byte, so they sit late by the cut in Serato and the row says so; MP3/AAC get a metadata-only grid in the rekordbox XML ("XML only", noted as not yet verified against the encoder delay; with the XML off, nothing is written; an MP3/AAC file without a grid, or whose grid needs review and was not confirmed, is skipped, since the XML would carry nothing for it, and so is one in Library mode whose grid was not confirmed) + "Convert to AIFF copy + cut") and **Library** (never change length; loudness only by default; rekordbox XML grid opt-in: the batch's XML lists a Library track only when its grid was confirmed in SoundCheck, because importing it replaces the DJ app's own grid; cut-to-grid only into a new folder). Switching files with Serato data to Library mode automatically (remembered per folder) arrives with the export panel.
5. **Export**: in place with backup (default) or to a folder; format source / AIFF / WAV 24-bit / FLAC; DJ-safe validation (44.1/48 kHz, integer PCM, stereo, plain PCM header, FORM AIFF); tags `TBPM` + `TXXX:BPM`, ReplayGain 2.0, `SOUNDCHECK` record; **rekordbox XML per batch** (carrier, with Analysis Lock advice): `soundcheck-rekordbox.xml` lists only files with a grid it may carry, each at its path now, spelled exactly as its folder lists the name (what the Finder, and rekordbox when it added the file, see: macOS opens a file under other Unicode spellings and cases too, and a FAT32 stick even reports back whichever spelling reached the file first, but rekordbox matches an existing track by its location, so any other spelling would add the track a second time), named by its own title and artist tags (the file name when it has no title), with one 4/4 `TEMPO` whose `Inizio` is the first beat in the file (after a Prepare cut, bar 1 at the lead), `Bpm` the written two-decimal BPM and `Battito` that beat's place in its bar; nothing else about the track is written (`Name`, `Artist`, `TotalTime`, `AverageBpm`, `Location` and the `TEMPO`, plus the XML's own `TrackID` that its playlist refers to; rekordbox matches the track by its location and keeps its own id), because **importing a track from the XML into rekordbox's collection overwrites that track's information, beat grid and BPM with the XML's: import one track first and check it before importing the rest**; a track whose grid needs review and was not confirmed, without a grid, or in a meter other than 4/4 is left out of the XML (importing it could only change its information, or clear rekordbox's own grid), and the report says why; its playlist is named after the batch (`SoundCheck 2026-10-11 14.05.33`), since rekordbox replaces a playlist of the same name on import; `grid-report.csv` (one row per file: file, mode, action, gain, cut, BPM, bar 1, what the XML carries or why not, grid check, notes; UTF-8 with a byte-order mark so spreadsheets show Turkish names; comma-separated with decimal points, so Excel set to a Turkish or other locale that uses `;` and decimal commas splits it wrongly when opened by double-click: import it through Data > From Text/CSV choosing comma and point, or open it in Numbers); both go into the export folder, or for an export in place into a new `~/Music/SoundCheck/exports/<date time>/` folder, and replace only files of those names that SoundCheck wrote (a foreign file of either name in the export folder refuses the export before any file is written); output self-check (`grid-check`); rekordbox USB exports refused with explanation; FLAC re-analysis notice; loudness tags a gain change makes stale are reported and carried unchanged: album Replay Gain, `RVA2`, iTunes `iTunNORM` (`COMM`), R 128 gains and Serato Autotags (auto gain); the track Replay Gain is replaced; the sidecar `<file>.soundcheck.json` of an exported file records the export settings, the plans, the grid as exported (bar 1 and the first bar line in the output's samples, the written BPM) and the source's loudness; a grid the user edited or confirmed is carried over to the exported file (bar 1 moved back by the cut), so a confirmed grid stays confirmed after the export, and the original's edit is kept beside it, so an undo brings it back; an export is refused, before anything is written, when the file changed between its plan and its write (for example, another export wrote it); Library keeps the file's modification time, Prepare does not; export waits for the start-up recovery, so no file is written while an interrupted change may still be repaired; a file's `SOUNDCHECK` record is read back when it is added, so a later run can see it was already processed (a record of a later format or a damaged one still marks the file as processed, with the reason it could not be read).
6. **Trust surface**: Action column (what will happen, in dB/LU/ms) separate from Status (where it is); "Done, verified" only after tiered verification; on-screen tag diff ("Verified: 27/27 identical, 3 added") with the cover rendered from the output file; Revert; three-line skip/error copy; positions-shift badge on Prepare rows.
7. **Batch UX**: drop folders/files, analyse-only, Needs-review queue (`N`/`Enter`/`Esc`), filter chips (`Cmd+F` filters by name), `Cmd+,` for the settings (it moves focus to the settings rail; in a window narrower than 1000 px the rail is a drawer, opened by `Cmd+,` or a toolbar button showing the target, such as `DJ · −11.0 LUFS`, the name filter folds into an icon that opens in the chips' place (a chip still filtering shows in it), and Add folder and Add tracks become one Add menu; a table narrower than 820 px, as in any window under about 1100 px, shows the true peak under the loudness and the meter under the BPM, so each row still fits without scrolling sideways), progress and cancel, resume from cache, per-batch CSV. The track list is remembered across restarts (analyses come back from the cache; a file moved or deleted since shows as "File not found"); **Clear list** empties it without touching any file.
8. **`sc-cli`** with `analyze <files>` (text, or `--json` with exactly the table's fields, one document per file; `--no-grid` for loudness only, `--bpm-range 70-180` for the DJ app's range, `--model small|full`, `--evidence` to include the model beats, activations and onsets a refit uses, `--no-cache`, `--jobs N` files at once with reports still printed in the order given), `plan <files>` (what processing would do to each file, decided exactly as the table decides it: `--mode dj|streaming`, `--target`, `--ceiling`; the Action, Status and review reasons per file, then the counts the filter chips show; grid edits saved in the app apply; `--batch-mode prepare|library` adds a line per file with what exporting would do: the gain, the head cut (`Cut 0.21 s`, where the cut falls after its move to the quietest frame, `Starts on bar 1`, `Starts on a bar line (bar 1 at 8.00 s)`, `Not cut: bar 1 1.00 s in`, `Not cut: grid needs review (bar 1 at 2.30 s; confirm it in the app)`, `length kept` in Library mode), the tags written (`BPM` in Prepare mode, Replay Gain 2.0 track gain and peak, the `SOUNDCHECK` record) and notes (a cut copy's Serato cues), or `XML only` with the reason (MP3/AAC until their writes arrive; with `--grid-only`, FLAC, sources other than 16- or 24-bit integer PCM and files without a tag), or "skipped" in three lines (what, why, what to do: Serato data found in the file's tags, or tags that could not be read to rule it out, and a cut in place, a sample rate other than 44.1/48 kHz, more than two channels, an analysed-only codec, silence, no grid, nothing to write with `--no-xml`); `--grid-only` plans no gain and no cut, `--lead-ms N` (0-50, default 5) sets the lead, `--no-xml` turns the batch's rekordbox XML off; with `--json` the outcome is the document's `export` field), `labels <files>` (evaluation labels from each file's grid as the app shows it, edits applied: `file,bpm,bar1_s,meter,grouping,confirmed,fit`, `fit` being `whole` or `start`; `--confirmed-only`), `bench <file>` (time and real-time factor of each stage), `eval --labels <csv> --dir <folder>` (scores BPM, meter, bar 1 (modulo the label's bar; only for rows with a labelled tempo; a row with `fit` `start` against the analysis refitted to the start) and integrated loudness against hand labels; `scripts/ffmpeg-reference.sh` writes the loudness reference), `cache path|clear` (the analysis cache lives under `~/Library/Caches/app.soundcheck.desktop/analysis`, keyed by the file's identity (inode and change time, not the disk, so a replugged USB stick keeps its analyses), size and time, settings and app version, and a file's entry is dropped whenever SoundCheck changes, undoes or recovers it, so a file SoundCheck rewrote at the same length and time, or put back by an undo, is analysed again), `apply <files> --gain-db <dB>` (WAV, AIFF, FLAC, one after the other in the order given; a file listed twice under any spelling (`T.wav`, `./T.wav`, its full path, another case) is changed once and its later mentions refused, and with `--out` two files that would get the same name there are caught before anything is written, only the first being written; the gain on every sample, a boost that would clip refused with the largest gain that would not; `--trim-samples N` cuts the start: the cut lands up to 1 ms before sample N per channel, on the quietest frame there (never after N), and the first 2 ms after it fade in from silence so the cut never clicks; the line gives the cut made and the one asked for (`500 frames trimmed (520 requested)`) and cue points and markers move by the cut made, `--bits 16|24` sets the output depth (default the source's, TPDF dither only at 16-bit), `--tag NAME=VALUE` (repeatable) adds or replaces a tag item by a name that is the same for every format: `BPM` (ID3 `TBPM` with the value rounded plus `TXXX:BPM` with the value as given in WAV/AIFF, `BPM` in FLAC), `INITIALKEY` (`TKEY` / `INITIALKEY`), any other name of capitals, digits and `_` (`TXXX:NAME` / `NAME`), written into a WAV/AIFF's existing ID3 chunk or a FLAC's Vorbis comment, never creating a tag; ID3v2.3/2.4 frame ids (`TBPM`, `TKEY`, and the iTunes `TCMP`, `GRP1`, ...) and labels with `:` are refused before any file is touched, while other four-letter names (`MOOD`, `YEAR`) are ordinary names; in place by default: the original is first copied to the backup folder, `~/Music/SoundCheck Backups/<local date>/<volume name>/<path on the volume>` (`--backup-root <dir>` or `SC_BACKUP_ROOT` chooses another; a backup is never overwritten), the new version is written next to it as a hidden temp file, read back and checked, then put in place atomically with the original's extended attributes (Finder tags, comments; macOS's own provenance stamp is the new file's, as for any file the app writes), creation date, permissions and modification time (`--no-keep-mtime` gives it the current time); `--out <dir>` writes copies there instead and leaves the files untouched, never replacing an existing one; next to each written file `<file>.soundcheck.json` records what was asked, what was carried, patched and added, the hashes of original and output and where the backup is (`--no-sidecar` skips it); one line per file, starting with the path as given: gain, depth, frames trimmed, blocks carried/patched, tags added or why not, stale loudness tags, "verified", the backup's path; a refusal prints three lines on stderr (what, why, what to do: rekordbox USB export, symbolic link, Finder-locked, read-only file or folder, access control list, other owner, hard links, not enough free space with the bytes needed and free, not DJ-safe, would clip, changed meanwhile, unsupported format, existing output); `--json` prints one document per file with `schema`, `file`, `ok`, the transaction id, output, backup, sidecar, the request, the render summary, BLAKE3 hashes, notes and per-step timings, or the error class, message, why and what to do), `undo <files>` (puts back the version before each file's newest change from the backup, read back and checked against its hash, with its metadata (what could not be restored is printed as a note), and says how many earlier changes remain; refused when the file changed since, so nothing is lost; the sidecar is removed, or rewritten for the change before; the backup is kept), `journal` (every change recorded in the backup folder's `journal.jsonl`, newest first: time, id, kind, how it ended or where it stopped, file, backup; `--incomplete` for unfinished ones only; `--forget <id>` gives up on a pending change so recovery leaves it alone from then on, deleting nothing and saying where its backup is; `--json`; JSON fields and their values are listed in the sc-cli README), `recover` (finishes or rolls back changes a crash or a pulled cable interrupted: before the new version was in place the temp file is removed and the original was never touched; after it, metadata and sidecar are completed; changes on a volume that is not mounted, or on a file another change is working on, stay pending and are tried again, and the exit code is then 3; the app runs it at start (what it found is in the app's log, `~/Library/Logs/app.soundcheck.desktop/soundcheck.log`) and `apply`/`undo` run it first), `process <files> --batch-mode prepare|library` (exports exactly as the app's Export button does, through the same engine job: recovery first, then per file the analysis (cached, grid edits applied), the plan worded as `plan --batch-mode` words it, the write in place after a backup or with `--out <dir>` as a copy, verified, refused before anything is replaced unless the output has the planned length (Library: unchanged; Prepare: shorter by the cut exactly), and a fresh analysis of the written file; `--grid-only`, `--depth source|16|24`, `--lead-ms N`, `--no-tbpm`, `--mode dj|streaming`, `--target`, `--ceiling`, `--json`; one line per file (`<path>: prepare: Gain -2.0 dB, Cut 0.21 s; tags ...; written and verified; backup <path>`, `XML only: ...`, or `skipped` with why and what to do) and a summary `N files: W written, X XML only, S skipped, F failed`; `--no-xml`; unless it, the batch's rekordbox XML and grid report are written into `--out` or a new `~/Music/SoundCheck/exports/<date time>/` folder and named in two lines), `xml <files> [--out FILE]` (the rekordbox XML of the files named, Library ones included since naming them is the choice: a grid edited or confirmed in the app is used first, else an exported file's grid read back from its sidecar while the file is unchanged, else the analysis; files without a grid it may carry are left out with the reason; to stdout, or to `--out`, which must end in `.xml` and never replaces a file SoundCheck did not write: an audio file a glob put there, or another app's XML, is refused in three lines and left as it is), `grid-check` (later). A file that fails prints an error document and the exit code is 2; a missing beat-tracking model stops the run before any file with the directories searched.

## Not in v0.1 (with the version that may bring it back)
True-peak limiter (with its gain-reduction meter), Re-Pitch, device picker, Serato BeatGrid GEOB (v0.2). Easy warp, AAC/M4A writes, Opus gain, SQLite cache, Chromaprint (v0.3). Key detection, pre-flight checks, per-platform penalty, Windows, updater, light theme (v0.4). Never: pitch correction, EQ, enhancement, copyleft or paid engines, filename suffixes, direct ANLZ/Engine DB writes.

## UX principles
1. Transparent by construction: the default path changes only gain; anything else is labelled with numbers.
2. Show, then act: analysis first, a table of what would happen, then Process.
3. One control per concern; correct defaults; advanced knobs exist but are quiet.
4. Never lose data: originals, metadata, journal, sidecar, Revert.
5. Fast enough to feel instant: >= 20x real time per core with the small model; cached re-runs in seconds.
6. Keyboard-first for the review loop (`N`, `Enter`, `1`-`4`, arrows, `Space`), mouse for everything else.
7. Fits half a laptop screen: the window can be as small as 720x560 px, beside Finder or a DJ app on a 1440 px screen. Below 1000 px wide the rails fold into drawers and the toolbars into icons; nothing is cut off or scrolls sideways.

## Open source
MIT OR Apache-2.0. No pricing, licence keys or trial logic. Every shipped dependency is permissive (MIT/Apache-2.0/MPL-2.0/BSD/ISC/Zlib). Public repo from the first commit; every tagged build signed and notarized (the owner holds an Apple Developer ID).

## Definition of done for v0.1
1. Clean M1 Mac: `git clone` -> `pnpm tauri dev` in < 15 min following the README; CI green on `ubuntu-latest` and `macos-14`.
2. 50 mixed files (WAV/AIFF/FLAC/MP3) analysed in < 3 min on an 8-core M1 with the small model (>= 20x real time per core).
3. EBU Tech 3341/3342 cases within tolerance under `SC_EBU_TESTSET=1`; integrated loudness within 0.1 LU of ffmpeg `ebur128` on 20 files.
4. 20-track evaluation set: BPM within +/-0.02 on >= 18/20 after at most one x2 / /2; after export, rekordbox 7 fresh analysis shows beat 1 as the first grid entry within 15 ms of the start, same phase and BPM, on >= 18/20 (hand-run release gate; octave flips count as failures unless the XML carrier pins them).
5. 12-file fixture set round-trips with byte-identical carried blocks for ID3v2.3/2.4 (APIC + GEOB), FLAC, AIFF, WAV; asserted in `cargo test`; crash-injection matrix passes.
6. MP3 `global_gain` output has the identical frame count and LAME header; a cue at sample N decodes to the same sample; undo restores the original hash.
7. Three DJs who are not the maintainer complete drop -> inspect -> fix one anchor -> export -> rekordbox without help.
8. GitHub Release `v0.1.0`: signed + notarized DMG with the small model, CHANGELOG, licence files, `cargo deny check` green.
