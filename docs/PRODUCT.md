# SoundCheck — product definition

Status: v0.1 scope fixed 2026-09-24. Open source. Source of truth for scope and UX.

## One sentence
SoundCheck levels a DJ library to one loudness and makes new tracks start exactly on the grid, so rekordbox, Serato and Traktor agree with it, without touching the sound or losing a single tag.

## Who it is for
1. **DJs** preparing new tracks and USB exports (rekordbox/CDJ, Serato, Traktor, Engine DJ) and levelling libraries they already cue-pointed.
2. **Producers and edit makers** who bounce at inconsistent levels and want a track that drops into rekordbox or Ableton at bar 1 with an exact BPM.
3. Streaming/podcast users (secondary): integrated-loudness presets.

## The three promises (and their tests)
1. **Same loudness** — gain only; when the ceiling would be hit the row says "Short by X LU". Test: EBU Tech 3341/3342 conformance; within 0.1 LU of ffmpeg `ebur128`.
2. **Fits the grid in rekordbox** — after export, rekordbox 7's own fresh analysis puts beat 1 at 0.000 s (+/-2 ms) with the same 2-decimal BPM on >= 18/20 tracks of the owner's labelled set; wrong tracks are fixed in the app in < 30 s.
3. **Nothing lost** — every tag block and the cover art carried byte-for-byte (proven by per-block SHA-256 fixtures and shown on screen), originals backed up, Revert available, filenames unchanged.

## What v0.1 does
1. **Analyse** WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3 (M4A/AAC/ALAC, Ogg/Opus analyse-only): loudness (I, max-M, max-S, **S-P95**, S-top30, LRA, true peak, PLR, timeline), beats/downbeats/BPM (0.01) with a three-state confidence and reason chips, **meter with grouping** (4/4, 3/4, 6/8, 9/8 aksak 2+2+2+3, 5/8, 7/8, 10/8), static-vs-drifts verdict with residual statistics, spec (rate/depth/channels/codec, DJ-safe flag), tags and cover art (read).
2. **Loudness fix** behind a top-level **DJ | Streaming** switch. DJ: one fixed, persisted **DJ target** on S-P95 (default -11 until measured; "Calibrate from my library" sets it once) and **Club hot** -8. Streaming: Spotify -14, Apple -16 on Integrated. Targets can be set from -30 to -4 LUFS and ceilings from -6 to 0 dBTP. Gain only, ceiling-capped (-0.5 dBTP DJ, -1.0 streaming), "Short by X LU" when headroom runs out; MP3 via lossless `global_gain` in 1.5 dB steps with the residual shown. Per-track override.
3. **Grid inspect and fix** per track (`Enter` or a double-click on an analysed row opens it, `Esc` returns with the row selected): waveform at beat zoom auto-located to bar 1 (the original in grey, the planned gain lighter inside it), bar ruler numbering every beat or pulse when zoomed in (9/8 2+2+2+3 reads `1 2 3 2 5 2 7 2 3`), grid overlay, residual lane (per bar / per beat), an overview strip of the whole track that moves the view, a cursor readout (time, bar and beat or pulse and group, residual, short-term loudness), the fit with its numbers coloured against the verdict limits, drift card with numbers; while playing, the waveform scrolls under the playhead once it reaches the middle of the view (with macOS Reduce motion it pages instead); in a window narrower than 1000 px the rail folds into a one-line fit strip above the waveform (confidence, BPM, meter, P95 and max, verdict) with the rest behind Details (`I`), and the toolbars keep their icons (Before / after, Beat 1 here) and the time signature; keys: `Space` play (from one beat before bar 1 the first time), `Home` back to bar 1, `C` click, `A` the analysed grid dashed under the edited one, `D` bar 1 at the pointer, `,` / `.` nudge, `1`-`9`, `T` tap, `M` meter, `R` reset, `Cmd+Z` / `Cmd+Shift+Z`, arrows pan, `+` / `-` zoom, `Enter` confirm and open the next Needs-review track, `N` next without confirming; fixes: drag anchor (kick-snapped), nudge +/-1 ms (10 ms, 1 beat), type/tap BPM, x2 / /2, `1`-`n` set which beat is beat 1, **fit to the whole track or its start** (offered when the tempo changes within the track: Start fits the grid to the opening from the first kick, up to 128 beats and fewer when the tempo already changes within them, where a DJ mixes in, each option showing the share of kicks it holds there; the rest still reads Drifts), meter picker (runner-up first), re-fit from here, reset, undo; ruler and click accents follow the grouping; **click audition** (accented downbeat) on a minimal in-app player, which plays the track at its planned gain (a settings change applies to the open track at once; the click follows that level and stays 6 dB over the music). Edits are saved (under `~/Library/Application Support/app.soundcheck.desktop/grid-edits`, so they survive restarts and a cleared cache) and apply while the audio is unchanged (a DJ app writing tags keeps them; another master of the track drops them). A grid the user confirms by ear leaves the Needs-review queue; its drift, residual and BPM-range marks stay on the row, and should a new analysis give a different grid for the same edit, the track returns to the queue.
4. **Two modes**: **Prepare** (new tracks; lossless files cut at the head to the first downbeat with at most one beat of pre-roll; in place allowed; MP3/AAC get a metadata-only grid + "Convert to AIFF copy + cut") and **Library** (never change length; loudness only by default; rekordbox XML grid opt-in; cut-to-grid only into a new folder). Auto-Library when Serato tags are present.
5. **Export**: in place with backup (default) or to a folder; format source / AIFF / WAV 24-bit / FLAC; DJ-safe validation (44.1/48 kHz, integer PCM, stereo, plain PCM header, FORM AIFF); tags `TBPM` + `TXXX:BPM`, ReplayGain 2.0, `SOUNDCHECK` record; **rekordbox XML per batch** (carrier, with Analysis Lock advice); `grid-report.csv`; output self-check (`grid-check`); rekordbox USB exports refused with explanation; FLAC re-analysis notice; stale Serato Autotags notice.
6. **Trust surface**: Action column (what will happen, in dB/LU/ms) separate from Status (where it is); "Done, verified" only after tiered verification; on-screen tag diff ("Verified: 27/27 identical, 3 added") with the cover rendered from the output file; Revert; three-line skip/error copy; positions-shift badge on Prepare rows.
7. **Batch UX**: drop folders/files, analyse-only, Needs-review queue (`N`/`Enter`/`Esc`), filter chips (`Cmd+F` filters by name), `Cmd+,` for the settings (it moves focus to the settings rail; in a window narrower than 1000 px the rail is a drawer, opened by `Cmd+,` or a toolbar button showing the target, such as `DJ · −11.0 LUFS`, the name filter folds into an icon that opens in the chips' place (a chip still filtering shows in it), and Add folder and Add tracks become one Add menu; a table narrower than 820 px, as in any window under about 1100 px, shows the true peak under the loudness and the meter under the BPM, so each row still fits without scrolling sideways), progress and cancel, resume from cache, per-batch CSV. The track list is remembered across restarts (analyses come back from the cache; a file moved or deleted since shows as "File not found"); **Clear list** empties it without touching any file.
8. **`sc-cli`** with `analyze <files>` (text, or `--json` with exactly the table's fields, one document per file; `--no-grid` for loudness only, `--bpm-range 70-180` for the DJ app's range, `--model small|full`, `--evidence` to include the model beats, activations and onsets a refit uses, `--no-cache`, `--jobs N` files at once with reports still printed in the order given), `plan <files>` (what processing would do to each file, decided exactly as the table decides it: `--mode dj|streaming`, `--target`, `--ceiling`; the Action, Status and review reasons per file, then the counts the filter chips show; grid edits saved in the app apply), `labels <files>` (evaluation labels from each file's grid as the app shows it, edits applied: `file,bpm,bar1_s,meter,grouping,confirmed,fit`, `fit` being `whole` or `start`; `--confirmed-only`), `bench <file>` (time and real-time factor of each stage), `eval --labels <csv> --dir <folder>` (scores BPM, meter, bar 1 (modulo the label's bar; only for rows with a labelled tempo; a row with `fit` `start` against the analysis refitted to the start) and integrated loudness against hand labels; `scripts/ffmpeg-reference.sh` writes the loudness reference), `cache path|clear` (the analysis cache lives under `~/Library/Caches/app.soundcheck.desktop/analysis`, keyed by file size and time, settings and app version), `apply <files> --gain-db <dB>` (WAV, AIFF, FLAC, one after the other in the order given; a file listed twice under any spelling (`T.wav`, `./T.wav`, its full path, another case) is changed once and its later mentions refused, and with `--out` two files that would get the same name there are caught before anything is written, only the first being written; the gain on every sample, a boost that would clip refused with the largest gain that would not; `--trim-samples N` cuts N samples per channel from the start, `--bits 16|24` sets the output depth (default the source's, TPDF dither only at 16-bit), `--tag NAME=VALUE` (repeatable) adds or replaces a tag item by a name that is the same for every format: `BPM` (ID3 `TBPM` with the value rounded plus `TXXX:BPM` with the value as given in WAV/AIFF, `BPM` in FLAC), `INITIALKEY` (`TKEY` / `INITIALKEY`), any other name of capitals, digits and `_` (`TXXX:NAME` / `NAME`), written into a WAV/AIFF's existing ID3 chunk or a FLAC's Vorbis comment, never creating a tag; ID3v2.3/2.4 frame ids (`TBPM`, `TKEY`, and the iTunes `TCMP`, `GRP1`, ...) and labels with `:` are refused before any file is touched, while other four-letter names (`MOOD`, `YEAR`) are ordinary names; in place by default: the original is first copied to the backup folder, `~/Music/SoundCheck Backups/<local date>/<volume name>/<path on the volume>` (`--backup-root <dir>` or `SC_BACKUP_ROOT` chooses another; a backup is never overwritten), the new version is written next to it as a hidden temp file, read back and checked, then put in place atomically with the original's extended attributes (Finder tags, comments), creation date, permissions and modification time (`--no-keep-mtime` gives it the current time); `--out <dir>` writes copies there instead and leaves the files untouched, never replacing an existing one; next to each written file `<file>.soundcheck.json` records what was asked, what was carried, patched and added, the hashes of original and output and where the backup is (`--no-sidecar` skips it); one line per file, starting with the path as given: gain, depth, frames trimmed, blocks carried/patched, tags added or why not, stale loudness tags, "verified", the backup's path; a refusal prints three lines on stderr (what, why, what to do: rekordbox USB export, symbolic link, Finder-locked, read-only file or folder, access control list, other owner, hard links, not enough free space with the bytes needed and free, not DJ-safe, would clip, changed meanwhile, unsupported format, existing output); `--json` prints one document per file with `schema`, `file`, `ok`, the transaction id, output, backup, sidecar, the request, the render summary, BLAKE3 hashes, notes and per-step timings, or the error class, message, why and what to do), `undo <files>` (puts back the version before each file's newest change from the backup, read back and checked against its hash, with its metadata, and says how many earlier changes remain; refused when the file changed since, so nothing is lost; the sidecar is removed, or rewritten for the change before; the backup is kept), `journal` (every change recorded in the backup folder's `journal.jsonl`, newest first: time, id, kind, how it ended or where it stopped, file, backup; `--incomplete` for unfinished ones only; `--forget <id>` gives up on a pending change so recovery leaves it alone from then on, deleting nothing and saying where its backup is; `--json`; JSON fields and their values are listed in the sc-cli README), `recover` (finishes or rolls back changes a crash or a pulled cable interrupted: before the new version was in place the temp file is removed and the original was never touched; after it, metadata and sidecar are completed; changes on a volume that is not mounted, or on a file another change is working on, stay pending and are tried again, and the exit code is then 3; the app runs it at start (what it found is in the app's log, `~/Library/Logs/app.soundcheck.desktop/soundcheck.log`) and `apply`/`undo` run it first), `process`, `grid-check`. A file that fails prints an error document and the exit code is 2; a missing beat-tracking model stops the run before any file with the directories searched.

## Not in v0.1 (with the version that may bring it back)
True-peak limiter, Re-Pitch, meters with ballistics, A/B audition, device picker, Serato BeatGrid GEOB (v0.2). Easy warp, AAC/M4A writes, Opus gain, SQLite cache, Chromaprint (v0.3). Key detection, pre-flight checks, per-platform penalty, Windows, updater, light theme (v0.4). Never: pitch correction, EQ, enhancement, copyleft or paid engines, filename suffixes, direct ANLZ/Engine DB writes.

## UX principles
1. Transparent by construction: the default path changes only gain; anything else is labelled with numbers.
2. Show, then act: analysis first, a table of what would happen, then Process.
3. One control per concern; correct defaults; advanced knobs exist but are quiet.
4. Never lose data: originals, metadata, journal, sidecar, Revert.
5. Fast enough to feel instant: >= 20x real time per core with the small model; cached re-runs in seconds.
6. Keyboard-first for the review loop (`N`, `Enter`, `1`-`4`, arrows, `Space`), mouse for everything else.
7. Fits half a laptop screen: the window can be as small as 720x560 px, beside Finder or a DJ app on a 1440 px screen. Below 1000 px wide the rails fold into drawers and the toolbars into icons; nothing is cut off or scrolls sideways.

## Definition of done for v0.1
1. Clean M1 Mac: `git clone` -> `pnpm tauri dev` in < 15 min following the README; CI green on `ubuntu-latest` and `macos-14`.
2. 50 mixed files (WAV/AIFF/FLAC/MP3) analysed in < 3 min on an 8-core M1 with the small model (>= 20x real time per core).
3. EBU Tech 3341/3342 cases within tolerance under `SC_EBU_TESTSET=1`; integrated loudness within 0.1 LU of ffmpeg `ebur128` on 20 files.
4. 20-track evaluation set: BPM within +/-0.02 on >= 18/20 after at most one x2 / /2; after export, rekordbox 7 fresh analysis shows beat 1 as the first grid entry within 15 ms of the start, same phase and BPM, on >= 18/20 (hand-run release gate; octave flips count as failures unless the XML carrier pins them).
5. 12-file fixture set round-trips with byte-identical carried blocks for ID3v2.3/2.4 (APIC + GEOB), FLAC, AIFF, WAV; asserted in `cargo test`; crash-injection matrix passes.
6. MP3 `global_gain` output has the identical frame count and LAME header; a cue at sample N decodes to the same sample; undo restores the original hash.
7. Three DJs who are not the maintainer complete drop -> inspect -> fix one anchor -> export -> rekordbox without help.
8. GitHub Release `v0.1.0`: signed + notarized DMG with the small model, CHANGELOG, licence files, `cargo deny check` green.
